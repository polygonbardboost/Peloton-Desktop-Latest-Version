## Features

- Detailed workout analytics tracking heart rate zones, cadence, elevation gain, and power output
- Interactive training calendar with customizable training plans for endurance, running, and cycling
- Seamless synchronization with external GPS sports watches, sensors, and fitness trackers
- Social connectivity features allowing friendly challenges, segment leaderboards, and route sharing
- Comprehensive health monitoring metrics including VO2 max estimates, recovery times, and sleep analysis
